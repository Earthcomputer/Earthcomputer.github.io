I went on a tour of 50 funny place names in Britain, from the map below, in a year.

![50 funny place names map](assets/IMG_20231225_151551.jpg){: lazy}

The rules were as follows:

- Do not use cars. That includes driving, taxis, or getting a lift. Other road vehicles such as buses are fine.
- Take at least one photo of the location.
- Buy at least one thing from a cafe, restaurant, or pub at the location.
  - If this is not possible, do so from a cafe, restaurant, or pub at a nearby location.
  - It should be an independent business, although this is often hard to verify on the spot so this rule is best-effort.

The rules were active from the moment I left my home in Rotherham to the moment I returned back home. Rotherham, in South Yorkshire, was a conveniently central location to be based for this tour.

#### 1. Penistone

7th January 2024
{: .date}

![Cafe in Penistone](assets/IMG_20240107_121810.jpg){: lazy}
![Penistone station](assets/IMG_20240107_123219.jpg){: lazy}
![View from Penistone station](assets/IMG_20240107_123954.jpg){: lazy}
{: .gallery}

One of the more famous places on this trip, Penistone was also the closest easily accessible location to my home in Rotherham, and therefore the one I went to first. It's actually quite a pretty market town in the Peak District.

I took the train from Rotherham to Meadowhall to Barnsley, where the plan was to take the train to Penistone. However, I missed this train, and ended up having to take a bus instead. It was a minibus service. I explored Penistone a little, although as it was a Sunday there wasn't much open. I bought a hot chocolate at the Loft Coffee Shop, then left by train for Rotherham.

Wigtwizzle was also relatively close to Rotherham and therefore easy to get to. It was a simple train to Sheffield followed by a bus to nearby Bolsterstone, and then a few kilometers of hiking around Broomhead Reservoir. Wigtwizzle itself is a small hamlet with little more than a few houses, so I got my J2O from a pub back in Bolsterstone.

#### 3. Butthole Lane

14th January 2024
{: .date}

![Cat on Butthole Lane](assets/IMG_20240114_112622.jpg){: lazy}
![Butthole Lane](assets/IMG_20240114_112913.jpg){: lazy}
![Meet'n'Eat cafe](assets/IMG_20240114_114431.jpg){: lazy}
{: .gallery}

Butthole Lane is a footpath in Shepshed, just west of Loughborough, Leicestershire, on the other side of the M1 motorway. I took the train down to Loughborough, and then the bus from Loughborough to Shepshed, and walked to Butthole Lane. I ate in the Meet'n'Eat cafe in Shepshed.
